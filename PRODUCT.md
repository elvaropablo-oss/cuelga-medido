# CuelgaMedido

CuelgaMedido converts a wall composition into physical marks measured from the floor and the left edge. It calculates one or two hooks, equal-frame rows and full grids while keeping hanger drop explicit.

Calculations stay local. The product must distinguish visual centre, frame edge and actual support point; it must never imply that it can choose a safe fixing for an unknown wall or weight. Mobile use beside the wall is a primary context.
